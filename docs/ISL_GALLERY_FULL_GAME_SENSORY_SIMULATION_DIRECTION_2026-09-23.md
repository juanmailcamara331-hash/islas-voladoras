# ISL · GALLERY FULL-GAME SENSORY SIMULATION · DIRECTION
Fecha: 2026-09-23
Estado: DIRECTION · LAB/PRESENTATION SURFACE · NO CANON · NO FALSE GAMEPLAY

## PURPOSE
Create a Gallery experience that lets a human feel the arc of the whole ISL game before every system is implemented.

This is NOT:
- fake gameplay;
- a replacement for playable evidence;
- a trailer pretending unfinished systems exist;
- a CANON authority engine.

This IS:
- a sensory simulation;
- a second-opinion tool;
- a visual/audio narrative map;
- a way to test whether the whole game feels coherent from beginning to late-game;
- a bridge between individual visual bullets and the lived promise of ISL.

## CORE FORMAT
A sequence of large immersive cards / chapters.

Each card may contain:
- one authoritative or clearly-labelled SENSOR image;
- short human-facing copy;
- music / ambient cue;
- optional subtle motion/parallax;
- one tiny interaction or choice;
- one visible consequence / transition;
- status label when needed: PLAYABLE / SENSOR / CONCEPT / PROTOTYPE / CANON DIRECTION;
- one question for the human tester.

No tool/debug language should dominate the experience.

## SENSORIAL LAYERS
VISUAL:
world scale, silhouettes, materiality, weather, light, impossible physics.

AUDIO:
music and ambience should change by chapter and function.
Audio is attached to scenes, not developed in isolation.

HUMAN:
relationships, humour, tenderness, fatigue, social life, consequence.

PLAY:
small verbs / choices only when they clarify the intended experience.

MEMORY:
later cards may visibly echo earlier choices, symbols, places or people.

## SECOND-OPINION MODE
For Carrillo / father / friends / blind testers:
- enter without internal methodology;
- experience cards in sequence;
- optional headphones;
- minimal explanation;
- reactions captured at natural breakpoints;
- ask what they remember, expect, fear, want to revisit, and what feels generic or unmistakably ISL.

Do NOT ask leading questions such as “do you like the Rick-like layer?”
Ask experiential questions:
- what kind of game do you think this becomes?
- where would you want to go next?
- what felt most alive?
- what felt fake/generic/confusing?
- what do you remember without looking back?
- what changed emotionally between beginning and late game?

## TRUTH LABELS
Every card must distinguish:
- REAL PLAYABLE;
- PROTOTYPE;
- STRONG SENSOR;
- CONCEPT;
- FUTURE DIRECTION.

No marketing smoke.

## QUALITY QUESTION
The simulation succeeds if a human can move through the entire arc and feel:
“this is one game becoming deeper and stranger”
rather than:
“this is a slideshow of unrelated cool concepts.”

## GUARDS
- PRIMARY remains Velaria V2 P0.
- HUMAN_DEVICE_GREEN remains PENDING.
- no card promotes CANON automatically;
- no SENSOR replaces playable evidence;
- no card may imply a mechanic already exists when it does not;
- no reference surface imitation;
- SAFE HARBOR unchanged;
- Gallery serves game truth, not the reverse.

## FUTURE IMPLEMENTATION
Prefer using existing Gallery / Command Center surfaces.
Do NOT create a new major subsystem if the current Gallery can host this through:
- chapter cards;
- audio controls;
- transitions;
- tags/status;
- optional feedback capture.

Build only after enough foundational bullets exist to make the sequence useful.

## REENCOUNTER ARC CROSS · 2026-09-24
Strong human-direction candidate:
“LA VIDA ES EL ARTE DEL REENCUENTRO.”

The full-game sensory arc should test whether ISL gradually transforms:
ARRIVAL / SEPARATION
→ CURIOSITY / WORLD STUDY
→ FRIENDSHIP / CREW
→ MISREADING / FRICTION
→ DISTANCE / LOSS
→ SELF-MADE MAPS / MODELS / EQUATIONS
→ RECOGNITION OF CHANGE
→ FAMILY / FRIEND RETURN
→ REUNION OR ACCEPTED SEPARATION
→ WORLD RE-READING.

This is not permission to make every chapter sentimental.
Humour, erotic tension, aggression, study, substances, punk irreverence, politics, science and absurdity may coexist, but each must serve character/world consequence rather than a checklist.

The protagonist should not have a “wellness optimization” arc. Her contradictory choices may accidentally produce care, damage, knowledge, connection or all four. Human/device testing should ask what players infer from consequences without telling them the intended lesson.

Memorial traces for father / deceased friend / uncle remain HUMAN TRACE lanes and may enter the sensory simulation only through dignified, provenance-linked, consent-aware mutation. No auto-publication and no forced literal likeness.
