# ISL Creation Process Ledger v0.1

## Purpose
Document not only what ISL becomes, but how it is made: decisions, discarded paths, bugs, reversals, evidence, tooling changes, human gates, experiments, external research abstractions, and why choices were made.

This is production evidence and future making-of material. It is not marketing copy and must not rewrite history.

## Capture model
For every meaningful iteration record:
- date/time
- checkpoint / commit / build
- problem or creative question
- hypothesis
- action taken
- evidence observed
- bug/regression if any
- why a path was kept, changed, or discarded
- human decision
- next experiment
- screenshots / clips / artifacts when useful
- cost/time avoided or added
- transferable lesson

## Making-of principle
Preserve:
- first ugly prototype
- wrong turns
- regressions
- surprising discoveries
- before/after screenshots
- moments where a user/friend changed direction
- automation that saved work
- places where human judgment overruled automation
- ideas that looked good but failed in play
- ideas that became better after contradiction

## Final documentary possibility
At project completion, the ledger may be transformed into:
- a short making-of film
- a timeline
- a behind-the-scenes interactive web page
- a postmortem
- a technical/creative case study

The documentary must be reconstructed from timestamped evidence, not retrospective invention.

## Privacy
Do not expose private chats, credentials, private identities, personal data, or tester details without explicit permission.
