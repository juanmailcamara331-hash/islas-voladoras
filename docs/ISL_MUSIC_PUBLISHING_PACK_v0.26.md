## TRACK STORIES

### 01 Above the Sleeping Coast
A quiet first horizon: a flying home glides over a sleeping coast while the day begins below. Adventure is present, but it does not need to shout.

### 02 Tea Above the Clouds
A pause above the cloudline. Tea, tools, notes and a ship that feels more like a home than a vehicle.

### 03 The Mahogany Deck
Morning on a deck polished by use, repairs and years of weather. Warm wood, small routines and the feeling of living between destinations.

### 04 Lanterns in the Mist
A city appears first through sound: birds, bells, breath and light inside fog. A first musical seed for Nácar de Niebla.

### 05 Where the Current Ends
The place where a current stops obeying the rules. Calm remains, but direction becomes uncertain and the sky feels slightly wrong.

### 06 A Waltz for the Slow Descent
The return after a long journey. Slow, intimate and tired, with enough warmth left to make the descent feel like coming home.
