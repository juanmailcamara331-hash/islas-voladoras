# ISL — MIDJOURNEY CAMPAIGN PROMPT BIBLE v0.23

## REGLA
No imitar artistas vivos ni copiar IP.
Describir composición, materiales, luz, emoción, lente y función.

## ESCENA BASE
A lived-in flying ship-home drifting above a sleeping coast of floating islands at dawn. The ship must feel inhabited, repaired, imperfect and loved. Visible ropes, patched sails, warm windows, small domestic objects, wind-worn wood and metal. Vast sky, believable scale, subtle traces of a world with memory. No generic steampunk, no airship cliché, no clean luxury vehicle.

## A · HYPERREAL WONDER
Photoreal cinematic fantasy, physically believable materials, volumetric dawn atmosphere, huge aerial depth, realistic cloud scattering, warm practical lights inside the ship, weathered timber and brass, patched fabric, subtle human traces, 35mm lens, grounded exposure, believable wind physics, awe without trailer bombast, highly detailed, natural color response.

## B · POETIC AUTHORIAL
Handcrafted cinematic fantasy illustration, layered paper-like surfaces, mineral pigments, imperfect edges, expressive atmospheric perspective, quiet symbolic details, warm domestic light against immense sky, elegant asymmetry, tactile and intimate, strange but readable, poetic without sentimentality.

## C · DARK SEPARATION
Adult dark fantasy, same ship-home and coast but reality is slightly misregistered: ropes pull toward incompatible horizons, shadows arrive early, distant islands align incorrectly, structural tension visible, twisted beauty, restrained dread, no gore, no horror cliché, physical mechanism failure, cold-warm contrast, cinematic realism.

## D · WARM CREW / HOME
Warm inhabited fantasy, deck at breakfast, mugs, drying clothes, tools, patched cushions, handwritten notes, half-repaired mechanisms, silhouettes of crew in casual routine, wind moving fabric, affectionate chaos, visual humor, believable lived-in mess, beautiful sky outside, intimate camera, no sitcom staging.

## NEGATIVES / CONTROL
avoid generic steampunk, avoid clean luxury airship, avoid pirate cliché, avoid floating rocks with no ecology, avoid excessive gears, avoid oversized fantasy weapons, avoid trailer poster composition unless campaign requires it.

## TEST VARIANTS
For each family change ONE:
- lens 24 / 35 / 50 mm;
- dawn / blue hour;
- exterior / window-interior;
- crew visible / no crew.
