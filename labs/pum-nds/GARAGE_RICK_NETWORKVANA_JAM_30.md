# GARAGE RICK · NETWORKVANA JAM 30

STATUS: PLAY MODE · GROUNDED · REVERSIBLE

## Purpose
Thirty minutes to get excited, make strange connections, act bigger than life, and still keep reality separate from metaphor.

## Rule
You may say:
- "I am going to win a Nobel"
- "I am the best physicist alive"
- "this tree sent me a signal"
- "this proves my theory"

inside PLAY MODE.

The Garage records these as:
FICTION / HYPOTHESIS / METAPHOR / MOTIVATIONAL ROLEPLAY

Never as established fact.

## 30-minute flow

### 0–5 · FLIPARSE
Say the impossible thing.
Exaggerate.
Rap it.
Make the claim huge.

Capture:
- exact phrase
- rhythm
- image
- body gesture
- one object if present

### 5–12 · PETAR LA NETWORKVANA
Cross it with at least three layers:
- game
- science
- psychiatry / medicine
- art
- campaign
- mechanics
- object
- interface
- friend problem
- music
- research

Do not force one category.

### 12–18 · MAKE ONE THING
Produce one cheap artifact:
- paragraph
- game mechanic
- diagram
- mockup
- tiny script
- campaign hook
- research question
- physical-object idea

### 18–24 · REALITY CHECK
For each strong claim mark:
FACT
INFERENCE
HYPOTHESIS
METAPHOR
JOKE

Example:
"the tree sent me a signal"
→ METAPHOR unless there is ordinary observable evidence for a real signal.

### 24–28 · SURVIVORS
Keep only the 1–3 things that still feel useful after the reality check.

### 28–30 · NEXT MOVE
Choose one:
BUILD
TEST
WRITE
ASK
PARK
KILL

## Short law
FLIPARSE IS ALLOWED.
CONFUSING FLIPARSE WITH PROOF IS NOT.

## Why this matters
The Garage protects the useful part of overreach:
- boldness
- imagination
- cross-layer connections
- humor
- motivation

while separating it from:
- unsupported scientific claims
- medical certainty
- claims about hidden messages
- automatic authority

## Networkvana
Working definition:
the temporary creative network created when one idea hits several layers at once and starts generating useful cross-connections.

NETWORKVANA is not evidence by itself.
It is a place to generate candidates.
