# ISL PUM · Human–AI Communication Protocol

Status: LAB ONLY · RESEARCH CANDIDATE

Goal:
Make interaction with AI feel like a playable creative instrument rather than prompt engineering.

## Surface actions
PIM = bring one thing in.
PAM = collide it with another.
PUM = generate/test a third possibility.
PARK = preserve without committing.
RETURN = revisit with new context.

## Hidden system
Every interaction may preserve:
- provenance
- timestamp
- source
- intent class
- human reaction
- downstream relations
- evidence level
- reversibility
- model/tool used

## Rule
AI can:
- suggest
- cross
- simulate
- compare
- compress
- remember relations
- prepare variants

AI cannot:
- silently promote canon
- infer private psychological truth from ambiguous signals
- erase provenance
- overwrite human decisions
- turn speculative theory into empirical fact

## Academic angle
Candidate research framing:
Can a small, playful command grammar reduce the cognitive overhead of interacting with complex AI systems while preserving human agency, provenance, and reversibility?

## Family mode
Non-experts should be able to contribute through:
- play
- voice
- drawing
- reactions
- gifts
- choices
- simple PIM/PAM/PUM actions

No one needs to know prompt engineering to matter.
