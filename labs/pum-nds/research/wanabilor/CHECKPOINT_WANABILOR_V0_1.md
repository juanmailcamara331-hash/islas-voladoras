## Current thesis seed
The interesting phenomenon is not exceptional capacity.

It is that a human brain can consciously:
- simulate possibilities;
- make associations;
- rehearse futures;
- reinterpret events;
- notice coincidences;
- play with meanings;
- compare imagination with experience;
- preserve some results and discard others.

Some apparent "hits" may arise from:
- ordinary prediction;
- memory;
- pattern recognition;
- inference;
- causal structure;
- chance;
- later reinterpretation.

The research interest is the process, not supernatural explanation.

## New candidate constructs
### 1. DELAYED SEMANTIC COLLAPSE
Keep several readings alive before forcing one meaning.

### 2. CORRECTION-AS-GENERATION
A correction can create a new branch, not merely fix an error.

### 3. NEGOTIATED EMBODIMENT
Voice, rhythm, gesture and movement are observable signals.
Their meaning is assigned/confirmed by the human.

### 4. EVIDENCE TOWEL
A physical/digital continuous record of what survives testing.

### 5. DOMINO PYRAMID
A useful working metaphor:
one event can trigger many different layers and consequences.
The useful unit may be the relation between pieces, not a single piece.
