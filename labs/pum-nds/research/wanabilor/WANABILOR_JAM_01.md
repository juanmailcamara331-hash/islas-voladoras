# WANABILOR JAM 01 · 15 MIN

STATUS: PLAY FIRST

## Goal
Make one weird thing and see where it propagates.

## Seed
Use one spontaneous phrase, object, gesture, image or sound.

## Do
1. Make 3 different readings.
2. Cross each with a different layer.
3. Make one tiny artifact from each.
4. Pick none immediately.
5. Wait.
6. Revisit later.
7. Keep only what still lives.

## Record only
- original seed
- three readings
- three artifacts
- what died
- what returned
- what surprised you
- what you kept

## Rule
No need to understand the theory while playing.
The trace can explain it later.
