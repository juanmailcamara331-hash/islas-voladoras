# WANABILOR · NEW RESEARCH CELL

STATUS: NEW · CLEAN START · HUMAN-GATED · PLAYFUL · EVIDENCE-AWARE

## Why this exists
This folder starts a fresh research cell for the recent line of work around:
- conscious simulation and association;
- creativity without exceptional-power claims;
- delayed semantic collapse;
- cross-layer survival;
- embodied interaction;
- evidence towel;
- playful metaphors that remain clearly separated from scientific claims.

It is intentionally separated from the older research corpus so it can grow without overwriting or retrofitting previous work.

## Working image
**A domino pyramid that can be played, knocked, rebuilt and learned from.**

One move may:
- fail locally;
- trigger another layer;
- generate a useful branch;
- become art, mechanics, paper, object, campaign or experiment;
- survive later review;
- or disappear.

No single layer owns the whole event.
