# GARAGE ZERO · LITERATURE-BACKED RESEARCH DELTA V0.1

STATUS: RESEARCH BACKBONE · SOURCE-AWARE · HUMAN-GATED

## 1. What the existing Garage corpus already had
The internal research lineage already established four unusually strong design claims:

1. PLAUSIBLE OUTPUT != UNDERSTOOD INTENT
2. OBSERVED BEHAVIOR != HIDDEN MENTAL TRUTH
3. NATURAL ACT FIRST, THEORY SECOND
4. THE DIFFERENCE BETWEEN HUMAN INTENT AND AI INTERPRETATION IS ITSELF A CO-CREATIVE OBJECT

The newer Garage work adds:
5. DO NOT COLLAPSE ONE INPUT INTO ONE CONSTRUCT
6. CROSS-LAYER RELATIONS CAN BE FIRST-CLASS CREATIVE OBJECTS
7. ONLY A SMALL FRACTION SHOULD SURVIVE INTO INTEGRATION
8. EMBODIED INPUT SHOULD BE LEARNED THROUGH EXPLICIT HUMAN CONFIRMATION, NOT MIND-READING

## 2. External research support

### 2010 · Tanagra
Smith, Whitehead, Mateas.
Mixed-initiative design can preserve direct human constraints while automation fills around them and rejects impossible configurations.
Garage relevance:
HUMAN ANCHOR -> MACHINE REBUILD AROUND IT.

### 2014 · Mixed-Initiative Co-Creativity
Yannakakis, Liapis, Alexopoulos.
Co-creativity is not simply automation; human and computational initiative coexist.
Garage relevance:
initiative can alternate rather than remain one-directional.

### 2014 · Creativity Support Index
Cherry & Latulipe.
Creativity-support tools can be evaluated along dimensions including:
- exploration
- expressiveness
- immersion
- enjoyment
- results worth effort
- collaboration
Garage relevance:
the Garage should not be judged only by output quality.

### 2015 · Drawing Apprentice
Davis et al.
Real-time human–AI abstract drawing can operate as an enactive collaborative process.
Garage relevance:
rough marks and incomplete acts can be a shared creative language.

### 2016 · Somaesthetic Appreciation Design
Höök et al.
Embodied interaction can be designed around bodily attention, rhythm and intimate correspondence between action and feedback.
Garage relevance:
body rhythm can be interaction material, but should not be overinterpreted.

### 2018 · Evaluating Co-Creative Systems
Karimi et al.
Evaluation needs to ask:
WHO evaluates?
WHAT is evaluated?
WHEN?
HOW?
Garage relevance:
evaluate process, relation and ownership, not only artifacts.

### 2019 · Co-Creative Interaction Framework
Guzdial & Riedl.
Human–AI co-creativity is fundamentally an interaction-design problem, not only a generation-quality problem.
Garage relevance:
the interface and initiative rhythm are part of the creative intelligence.

### 2020 · Real-Time Creative AI
McCormack et al.
Real-time co-creation depends on trust, two-way communication, shared awareness, conflict, error, evaluation and flow.
Garage relevance:
timing, waiting, silence and interruption are first-class design variables.

### 2022 · COFI
Rezwana & Maher.
Across 92 systems, turn-taking, contribution type and communication are core dimensions of human–AI co-creative interaction.
Garage relevance:
interaction policy should be explicit and testable.

### 2024 · Co-Creation and Self-Efficacy
McGuire, De Cremer, Van de Cruys.
Interface design changes creative outcomes; treating the human primarily as an editor can harm creativity relative to co-creator configurations.
Garage relevance:
HUMAN SHOULD NOT BECOME CLEANUP OPERATOR.

### 2025 · LLM Multi-Agent Creativity Survey
Lin et al.
Creative multi-agent systems use divergent exploration, iterative refinement and collaborative synthesis, while suffering from coordination and evaluation problems.
Garage relevance:
MORE AGENTS != MORE CREATIVITY.
Differentiated functions + coordination + human gate matter.

## 3. What appears genuinely distinctive in the current Garage direction

The novelty candidate is NOT:
"body input is creative."
That exists.

It is NOT:
"AI and human co-create."
That exists.

The more distinctive candidate is the combination:

NATURAL EMBODIED ACT
-> MULTIMODAL TRACE
-> MULTIPLE CANDIDATE FUNCTIONS
-> CROSS-LAYER PRESERVATION
-> HUMAN CORRECTION / REDEFINITION
-> DELAYED SURVIVOR TEST
-> PARTIAL INTEGRATION

with explicit preservation of:
- rejected branches
- AI misreadings
- human correction
- provenance
- cross-layer transformations
- later reuse

## 4. Strong research proposition

A creative system may gain value by delaying semantic collapse.

Working proposition:

**DELAYED SEMANTIC COLLAPSE can preserve cross-layer creative affordances that would be lost by early classification.**

Operationally:
EARLY CLASSIFICATION condition:
one input -> one assigned function/category.

DELAYED COLLAPSE condition:
one input -> several provisional functions/layers -> small tests -> delayed human selection.

This is testable.

## 5. Second research proposition

**CORRECTION IS GENERATIVE, NOT MERELY REPARATIVE.**

Human correction can:
- reject AI assumptions
- expose normative attractors
- create alternate branches
- reveal missing representation
- change future interaction mappings

This extends the internal relation paper:
AI MISUNDERSTANDING -> HUMAN CORRECTION -> DELTA
into:
DELTA -> NEW CREATIVE POSSIBILITY.

## 6. Third research proposition

**EMBODIED SIGNALS SHOULD BE NEGOTIATED, NOT DECODED.**

The system measures:
- timing
- rhythm
- gesture
- movement
- acoustic features

The human negotiates/assigns:
- meaning
- command
- value
- retention

This avoids the false claim:
SIGNAL -> TRUE EMOTION.

Instead:
SIGNAL -> CANDIDATE MAPPING -> HUMAN CONFIRMATION -> LEARNED INTERACTION.

## 7. Candidate equation family

Let x be an input event.
Let L = {l1 ... ln} be possible layers.
Let f(x, li) be candidate usefulness in layer li.
Let C(li, lj) be a cross-layer relation.
Let H be human correction/selection.
Let E be later experience.

Do not compute a universal creativity score.

Use instead a survivor relation:

S(x) = H(E({f(x,li), C(li,lj)}))

where S(x) is the subset that survives delayed human evaluation.

Creative value may arise from a cross-layer edge:
C(li, lj)
even when f(x, li) or f(x, lj) alone is weak.

This formalism is deliberately minimal and should not be treated as validated mathematics until empirically tested.

## 8. What to test next
Do not build more theory before testing these three claims:

A. DELAYED COLLAPSE vs EARLY CLASSIFICATION
B. CORRECTION-AS-REPAIR vs CORRECTION-AS-GENERATOR
C. FIXED BODY MAPPING vs NEGOTIATED BODY MAPPING
