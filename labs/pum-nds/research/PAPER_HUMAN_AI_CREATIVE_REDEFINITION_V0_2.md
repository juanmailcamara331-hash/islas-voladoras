# PAPER A · HUMAN–AI CREATIVE REDEFINITION · V0.2 BACKBONE

## Working title
**Delayed Semantic Collapse in Human–AI Co-Creation: Embodied Trace, Correction and Cross-Layer Creative Survival**

## One-sentence thesis
Human–AI creative systems may preserve more useful possibilities when they delay semantic collapse, treat correction as generative evidence, and let embodied signals acquire meaning through explicit human negotiation.

## Problem
Most generative interfaces tend to convert an input into an output or task class quickly.
That is efficient, but may erase:
- alternate meanings
- cross-domain affordances
- rejected-but-useful branches
- productive AI misunderstandings
- later reinterpretations

## Proposed model
NATURAL ACT
-> OBSERVABLE TRACE
-> MULTIPLE PROVISIONAL READINGS
-> CROSS-LAYER TESTS
-> HUMAN CORRECTION
-> DELAY
-> SURVIVOR SELECTION
-> PARTIAL INTEGRATION

## Three contributions
1. DELAYED SEMANTIC COLLAPSE
Do not force one interpretation too early.

2. CORRECTION-AS-GENERATION
Treat human correction as a source of new creative structure, not only error repair.

3. NEGOTIATED EMBODIMENT
Use bodily/acoustic traces as measurable interaction channels whose meanings are learned through human confirmation.

## Relation to prior work
Tanagra and mixed-initiative systems establish shared initiative.
Drawing Apprentice establishes real-time enactive co-creation.
Somaesthetic interaction establishes bodily interaction as design material.
Karimi et al. establish process-sensitive evaluation.
Guzdial/Riedl and COFI establish interaction structure as a research object.
McCormack et al. foreground real-time communication, error and flow.
Recent empirical work shows interface roles affect creativity.
Multi-agent surveys show divergence/refinement/synthesis but expose coordination problems.

The proposed extension is to make delayed cross-layer preservation, correction trajectories and delayed survivor selection explicit first-class mechanisms.

## Falsifiable version
If early classification performs equally or better on survivor rate, reuse, ownership and effort, delayed collapse is not justified as a default.

## Evaluation
Compare:
- early single-class routing
- delayed multi-layer routing

Use:
- Creativity Support Index dimensions

## Epistemic boundary
Do not claim:
- access to unconscious content
- objective emotion inference
- quantum explanation of creativity
- universal psychological mechanism

May claim only what observed behavior and controlled comparison support.
