# SACARSE EL RABO · JAM 01

STATUS: PLAY FIRST · TRACE EVERYTHING USEFUL

## 15-minute mode
1. Say or make one ridiculous, ambitious or strange thing.
2. Keep 3–5 possible readings alive.
3. Cross them with different layers.
4. Materialize at least one tiny artifact.
5. Correct freely.
6. Do not choose the grand theory yet.
7. Revisit later.
8. Keep only what still lives.

## Can include
Nobel-level exaggeration as roleplay; bizarre metaphors; body rhythm; microphone; banana; chain; drawing; game mechanic; paper question; thermal evidence receipt.

## Must distinguish
PLAY != PROOF

## Minimum trace
Original seed; observable act; candidate readings; artifacts; correction; delayed survivor; evidence for; evidence against; next move.

## Success
Success is not "the idea was true." Success is: something useful was made; the trace is recoverable; the human still owns the meaning; the test taught us something.
