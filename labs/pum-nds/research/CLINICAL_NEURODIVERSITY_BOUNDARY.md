# ISL PUM · Clinical / Neurodiversity Boundary

Status: SAFETY + RESEARCH BOUNDARY

## Allowed now
Use unusual subjective experience, associative richness, salience, metaphor, liminality and perspective shifts as:
- art source;
- philosophical material;
- game mechanics;
- phenomenological self-report;
- exploratory research questions.

## Not allowed as automatic inference
The game must not diagnose or classify:
- schizophrenia;
- autism;
- mania;
- anxiety;
- depression;
- intelligence;
- "superintelligence";
- true emotional state.

Gesture, voice, play style and drawing may produce game-state features, never medical conclusions by themselves.

## Future clinical research
Any hospital/clinical study requires:
- clinical collaborators;
- ethics/IRB-equivalent approval where applicable;
- informed consent;
- privacy/data minimization;
- validated measures;
- predefined outcomes;
- adverse-event procedures;
- explicit separation between exploratory and diagnostic claims.

## Neurodiversity language
Do not invent a universal replacement label for autistic people.
Respect individual/community language preferences and study terminology rather than imposing a new identity label from the game.

## Chemistry/fabrication
Creative combination mechanics may inspire fictional or design concepts.
Any real chemical/material claim requires domain expertise, safety review and empirical validation before fabrication.

## Rule
Meaningful experience is valid as experience.
Its explanatory theory remains a hypothesis until supported independently.
