# ISL PUM · Prior Art Landscape

Status: RESEARCH SUPPORT · NOT A NOVELTY CLAIM

## What clearly has precedent
- Biofeedback-adaptive games exist. Nevermind adapted gameplay using physiological/emotional signals and eye tracking.
- Affect-adaptive games are an established research field, but systematic reviews report inconsistent theory, methods and evaluation quality.
- Player modeling + procedural content generation + adaptive storytelling have existing research precedents.
- Mixed-initiative / co-creative game design systems exist.
- Gesture/touch/microphone-based game mechanics have extensive precedent, especially on Nintendo DS and mobile.
- Player telemetry as a design input is established.

## What appears less common as a single integrated system
The candidate novelty is NOT any single component. It is the integrated pipeline:

portable retro RPG
+ multimodal expressive input
+ bounded/adaptive serendipity
+ provenance-preserving object/history model
+ blind player run
+ compact playtrace
+ human-gated interpretation
+ branch simulation
+ cross-device save continuity
+ export into a creative operating system
+ structured handoff into Unreal production
+ preserved research provenance/reproducibility.

This combination should be treated as a hypothesis of novelty until a formal literature and prior-art review is completed.

## Closest conceptual families
1. Affective / biofeedback games.
2. Dynamic difficulty and AI directors.
3. Personalized procedural content generation.
4. Mixed-initiative co-creative systems.
5. Player modeling from telemetry.
6. Tangible/gesture/multimodal interaction.
7. Game analytics for design iteration.
8. Cross-engine prototyping / rapid vertical-slice validation.
9. Human-AI co-creation with provenance.

## Academic claim discipline
Never write:
"Nobody has ever done this."

Prefer:
"We found prior work on the constituent components, while the specific integration examined here appears comparatively underexplored."

Novelty must be supported by:
- explicit search strategy;
- databases;
- inclusion/exclusion criteria;
- comparison matrix;
- cited closest systems;
- reproducible search log.

## Research opportunity
A strong paper may focus less on "new mechanic" and more on:
- architecture of integration;
- preservation of human agency;
- provenance and remanence;
- transfer from low-cost play probe to high-cost production engine;
- empirical evaluation of whether the pipeline improves design decisions, fun, or iteration speed.
