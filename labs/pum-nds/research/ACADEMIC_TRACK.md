# ISL PUM · Academic Track

Status: RESEARCH CANDIDATE · NOT SCIENTIFIC CLAIM · HUMAN-GATED

## Principle
ISL may be artistically extreme. The academic track must remain conservative:
- distinguish observation, hypothesis, interpretation, and fiction;
- never treat subjective experience or lore as evidence of external physical phenomena;
- preserve raw data, code version, protocol version, and analysis version;
- preregister confirmatory hypotheses when practical;
- keep exploratory and confirmatory analyses separate;
- never infer psychological diagnosis or "true emotion" from play/voice data.

## Candidate research questions
RQ1. Can a compact retro-game prototype act as a low-cost design probe for later high-cost Unreal systems?
RQ2. Can persistent provenance + remanence + bounded serendipity produce useful creative candidates without sacrificing human authority?
RQ3. Can object-history systems produce more meaningful player attachment than rarity/stat labels alone?
RQ4. Can multimodal input (buttons, gesture/drawing, optional acoustic features) increase expressive play without reducing accessibility?
RQ5. Can playtrace data help reveal design preferences more reliably than post-hoc verbal preference alone, while remaining non-diagnostic?
RQ6. Can a human-gated pipeline convert observed play into reusable design records, Unreal-ready data structures and reproducible prototypes?

## Evidence ladder
E0 FICTION / LORE
E1 PERSONAL NOTE / INTUITION
E2 OBSERVED SINGLE-RUN GAME BEHAVIOR
E3 REPEATED WITH SAME PLAYER
E4 MULTI-PLAYER OBSERVATIONAL
E5 CONTROLLED COMPARISON / A-B
E6 PREREGISTERED CONFIRMATORY STUDY
E7 EXTERNAL REPLICATION

No claim may be promoted above its evidence level.

## Data integrity
Preserve:
- exact ROM hash;
- exact save hash;
- build commit;
- device/emulator version;
- protocol version;
- extractor version;
- analysis notebook/version;
- exclusions;
- failures;
- null results.

## Voice
Allowed research variables:
- loudness/energy;
- pitch-related features;
- timing/rhythm;
- duration;
- silence;
- coarse spectral descriptors.

Forbidden shortcut:
"voice feature = true emotion/mental state".

If affect is studied later, use explicit self-report and validated experimental design rather than treating acoustic features as ground truth.

## Gesture/drawing
Store:
- path geometry;
- duration;
- velocity;
- acceleration;
- direction changes;
- repetition;
- context;
- in-game consequence.

Do not equate gesture features directly with anxiety, calmness, aggression or personality.

## Theory integration rule
Any future "theory of everything" material must enter as one of:
- PHILOSOPHICAL FRAME;
- DESIGN METAPHOR;
- FORMAL MODEL;
- EMPIRICAL HYPOTHESIS.

Never merge these categories silently.

A formal model must define terms and predictions.
An empirical hypothesis must identify measurable variables and possible falsification.
A philosophical frame may guide design without being presented as empirical proof.

## Human authority
Academic novelty is not declared by the system.
It is tested against prior literature, evidence, reproducibility and external review.
